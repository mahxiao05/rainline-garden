    What to plant in October in Oregon and Washington

    Garlic month. The first frost reaches most of the west side by early November, so finish harvesting tender crops. Up to 7 of our plants can go in somewhere in the Northwest this month.

    What to plant in October, by region

    West-side valleys (Portland): plant garlic, fava beans, shallots, tulips, daffodils, camas and peonies outside.

    Coast (Astoria): plant garlic, fava beans, shallots, tulips, daffodils, camas and peonies outside.

    Columbia Basin and eastern valleys (Pendleton): plant garlic, shallots, tulips, daffodils, camas and peonies outside.

    High desert (Bend and Redmond): plant garlic, shallots, tulips, daffodils, camas and peonies outside.

    Inland Northwest (Spokane): plant garlic, shallots, tulips, daffodils, camas and peonies outside.

    October garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Plant garlic cloves, pointed end up, about 2 inches deep. West side
    • Plant shallots and overwintering onion sets.
    • Plant tulips, daffodils and other spring bulbs.
    • Sow fava beans and cover crops in empty beds. West side

    Start indoors

    • Pot up a few herbs like parsley and chives to grow on a windowsill.

    Harvest

    • Winter squash and pumpkins before a hard frost, then cure them somewhere warm.
    • Green tomatoes before frost; many will ripen indoors in a paper bag.
    • Carrots, beets, leeks and fall greens. Carrots sweeten after a light frost.
    • The last potatoes, on a dry day.

    Garden care

    • Rake leaves onto beds or into a compost pile as winter mulch.
    • Lift dahlia tubers after frost blackens the leaves, or mulch them heavily where winters are mild.
    • Spread compost or lime on empty beds so winter rain works it in.
    • Clean and store tomato cages and stakes.

    Pests and problems

    • Clear out squash and cucumber vines with mildew.
    • Keep slug traps out around fall greens. West side

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Plant fruit trees and shrubs in the fall rains. West side
    • Mulch strawberries after a few frosts.
    • Cut back asparagus ferns once they turn yellow.
