    September garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Plant a cover crop, such as crimson clover or cereal rye, in emptied beds.
    • Sow spinach and mâche early in the month for overwintering. West-side valleys
    • Plant garlic late in the month. High desert and Inland Northwest
    • Plant spring bulbs like tulips and daffodils late in the month. East-side valleys

    Start indoors

    • Sow a tray of lettuce or herbs for a sunny windowsill.

    Harvest

    • Winter squash once the rind is hard and the stem is dry; cure it somewhere warm for two weeks.
    • Tomatoes, peppers, beans and the last corn.
    • Fall-bearing raspberries and late blueberries.
    • Pick everything tender before the first frost. High desert and Inland Northwest

    Garden care

    • Pinch off new tomato flowers so the plant ripens fruit already on the vine.
    • Cover tender crops on cold nights; watch the forecast. High desert and Inland Northwest
    • Cut back watering as days cool, but don't let fall greens dry out.
    • Write down what grew well and what didn't while you remember.

    Pests and problems

    • Pull and bag tomato and potato vines with late blight; don't compost them.
    • Slugs come back with the fall rains. West-side valleys

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Plant and divide perennials; fall rain gets them established. West-side valleys
    • Plant new strawberry plants or move runners into place.
    • Lift and dry onion sets you'll replant.
