    June jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Sow a second round of beans, summer squash and cucumbers for late-summer harvests.
    • Sow carrots and beets for a midsummer crop.
    • Plant warm crops early in the month once frost risk is past. High desert and Inland Northwest

    Start indoors

    • Start fall broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower and kale indoors or in a shaded nursery bed.

    Harvest

    • Peas, lettuce and spinach before summer heat makes them bolt.
    • Garlic scapes on hardneck garlic: snap them off and eat them.
    • Strawberries and the first zucchini.
    • Early potatoes once the plants flower.

    Garden care

    • Water deeply about an inch a week, more in the eastern valleys and in sandy soil.
    • Keep tying tomatoes and pinching suckers on vining types.
    • Side-dress heavy feeders like corn, squash and brassicas with compost or fertilizer.
    • Stop cutting asparagus and let the ferns grow.

    Pests and problems

    • Look for aphids on new growth; a hard spray of water knocks them back.
    • Watch for powdery mildew on squash as days warm.
    • Keep netting on carrots and brassicas.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Water blueberries regularly; they're shallow-rooted.
    • Deadhead roses and annual flowers to keep them blooming.
    • Stop harvesting rhubarb by late June so the plant can rebuild.
