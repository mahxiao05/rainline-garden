    March jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Sow peas, radishes, spinach and arugula once the soil can be worked. West-side valleys
    • Plant onion sets, potatoes (late in the month) and asparagus crowns. West-side valleys
    • Sow peas and fava beans as soon as the soil dries out. Coast

    Start indoors

    • Start tomatoes, peppers and eggplant six to eight weeks before your warm-crop planting date.
    • Start broccoli, cabbage and kale indoors. East-side valleys
    • Start onions and leeks indoors. High desert and Inland Northwest

    Harvest

    • Pick overwintered kale before it flowers; the flower buds are good to eat too. West-side valleys
    • Cut the first rhubarb stalks late in the month. West-side valleys

    Garden care

    • Spread an inch or two of compost on beds ahead of planting.
    • Buy a soil thermometer. It tells you when crops can go in better than the calendar.
    • Test soil pH if you haven't for a few years.
    • Harden off cool-crop seedlings for a week before planting them out.

    Pests and problems

    • Northwest slugs wake up hungry. Bait with iron phosphate or use traps around seedlings. West-side valleys
    • Cover radishes, turnips and brassicas with row cover at planting to keep root maggot flies off.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Plant strawberries, blueberries and raspberries. West-side valleys
    • Feed blueberries with an acid fertilizer as buds swell.
    • Divide crowded perennials and chives.
