    May in the garden

    Warm crops go out once the soil warms. West of the Cascades that's usually mid to late May; in the high desert it's still risky.

    May jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Plant tomatoes, peppers and squash once nights stay above 50°F and the soil is warm. West-side valleys
    • Sow beans, corn and cucumbers when the soil reaches about 60°F.
    • Plant warm crops early in the month. East-side valleys
    • Transplant warm crops late in the month with frost cover ready. High desert and Inland Northwest
    • Keep sowing lettuce, radishes and carrots every two to three weeks.
    • Sow basil outside, or set out basil starts, once nights are warm.

    Start indoors

    • Start fall broccoli, cabbage and Brussels sprouts late in the month for summer transplanting.

    Harvest

    • Lettuce, spinach, radishes, peas (late in the month) and green onions.
    • Strawberries start late in the month. West-side valleys
    • Asparagus until the spears get thin.

    Garden care

    • Mulch around plants after the soil warms, to hold moisture and smother weeds.
    • Thin carrots and beets sown in spring.
    • Stake or cage tomatoes at planting time.
    • Start regular watering as rain tapers off; water deeply, not often.

    Pests and problems

    • Cover young squash and cucumbers with row cover until they flower, to stop cucumber beetles.
    • Check the undersides of brassica leaves for cabbage worm eggs and caterpillars.
    • Hand-pick slugs on damp evenings. West-side valleys

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Pinch out the first strawberry runners so plants put energy into fruit.
    • Net blueberries and cherries before they ripen; birds find them fast.
    • Plant out dahlias and other summer bulbs.
