    What to plant in December in Oregon and Washington

    Rest and review. Pick hardy greens on mild days and enjoy the stored harvest.

    What to plant in December, by region

    West-side valleys (Portland): nothing goes in this month.

    Coast (Astoria): nothing goes in this month.

    Columbia Basin and eastern valleys (Pendleton): nothing goes in this month.

    High desert (Bend and Redmond): nothing goes in this month.

    Inland Northwest (Spokane): nothing goes in this month.

    December garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Nothing to plant outside. Bare-root season starts at nurseries late in the month. West side

    Start indoors

    • Grow microgreens or pea shoots on a sunny windowsill.

    Harvest

    • Kale, leeks and other hardy crops as needed. West side
    • Stored squash, potatoes, onions and garlic.

    Garden care

    • Cover winter greens with row cover on nights below the low 20s. West side
    • Review this year's notes: what thrived, what failed and which varieties to repeat.
    • Clean, oil and store tools.
    • Shake heavy snow off shrubs and cold frames. East side

    Pests and problems

    • Check stored produce for rot and mice.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Wait to prune fruit trees until late winter, when the worst cold has passed.
    • Water evergreens and containers before a hard freeze if the soil is dry. East side
