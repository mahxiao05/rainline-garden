    December in the garden

    Rest and review. Pick hardy greens on mild days and enjoy the stored harvest.

    What to plant in December

    Nothing goes in the ground or into seed trays anywhere in the region in December.

    December jobs

    Start indoors

    • Grow microgreens or pea shoots on a sunny windowsill.

    Harvest

    • Kale, leeks and other hardy crops as needed. West-side valleys
    • Stored squash, potatoes, onions and garlic.

    Garden care

    • Cover winter greens with row cover on nights below the low 20s. West-side valleys
    • Review this year's notes: what thrived, what failed and which varieties to repeat.
    • Clean, oil and store tools.
    • Shake heavy snow off shrubs and cold frames. East-side valleys

    Pests and problems

    • Check stored produce for rot and mice.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Wait to prune fruit trees until late winter, when the worst cold has passed.
    • Water evergreens and containers before a hard freeze if the soil is dry. East-side valleys
