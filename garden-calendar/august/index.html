    August garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Set out fall and winter crop starts: kale, broccoli, cabbage, collards. West-side valleys
    • Sow spinach, lettuce, arugula, radishes and bok choy for fall.
    • Sow overwintering onions late in the month. West-side valleys
    • Sow fall greens early in the month. High desert and Inland Northwest

    Start indoors

    • Start lettuce and other greens in trays if the ground is too hot for them to sprout.

    Harvest

    • Tomatoes, peppers, corn, beans and squash. Pick often to keep plants producing.
    • Potatoes once the vines die back.
    • Onions once the tops fall over; cure them for two weeks before storing.
    • Melons when they slip easily from the vine. East-side valleys

    Garden care

    • Keep watering through dry spells, especially new fall plantings.
    • Plan for overwintering crops west of the Cascades, like kale, leeks and overwintering cauliflower. West-side valleys
    • Save seed from open-pollinated beans and tomatoes.

    Pests and problems

    • Late blight risk rises as nights cool and dew gets heavier. West-side valleys
    • Keep brassica starts under netting; cabbage moths are at their peak.
    • Yellowjackets get bold around ripe fruit; pick fruit promptly.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Prune summer-fruiting raspberries if you haven't.
    • Take strawberry runners to make new plants for next year.
    • Order spring bulbs like tulips and daffodils for fall planting.
