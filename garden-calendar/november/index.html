    November garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Finish planting garlic early in the month. West-side valleys
    • Plant bare-root roses and fruit trees when they arrive at nurseries. West-side valleys

    Start indoors

    • Nothing to start yet. Take stock of leftover seed and note what to reorder.

    Harvest

    • Kale, leeks, cabbage, Brussels sprouts and root crops as needed. West-side valleys
    • Bring in the last carrots and beets before the ground freezes. East-side valleys

    Garden care

    • Mulch garlic and overwintering crops with straw or leaves.
    • Drain and store hoses before hard freezes. East-side valleys
    • Test soil pH. Most Northwest soils run acidic and benefit from lime spread over winter. West-side valleys
    • Cover bare beds with leaves, a tarp or a cover crop so rain doesn't compact them.

    Pests and problems

    • Clean up diseased plant debris; don't compost blighted tomato vines.
    • Check stored squash and potatoes every couple of weeks for rot.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Mulch blueberries, raspberries and perennials before hard freezes.
    • Wrap young fruit tree trunks to keep voles and rodents off. East-side valleys
