    April garden jobs

    Plant and sow

    • Set out broccoli, cabbage, kale and lettuce starts. West-side valleys
    • Sow carrots, beets, chard and more lettuce and radishes every couple of weeks.
    • Sow peas, spinach and radishes and plant potatoes. East-side valleys
    • Sow peas, radishes, lettuce and spinach late in the month. High desert and Inland Northwest
    • Plant potatoes and onion sets. Coast

    Start indoors

    • Start tomatoes and peppers now if you haven't. High desert and Inland Northwest
    • Start cucumbers, squash and melons indoors three to four weeks before planting out.
    • Pot tomato seedlings up into bigger containers so they don't stall.

    Harvest

    • Radishes, spinach and the first lettuce thinnings. West-side valleys
    • Asparagus spears from beds three or more years old.
    • Rhubarb stalks.

    Garden care

    • Keep row cover handy. Late frosts are common even after your typical last frost date.
    • Harden off seedlings with a few hours outdoors each day, longer as the week goes on.
    • Thin seedlings once they have true leaves, so the rest have room.
    • Set up drip lines or soaker hoses before plants get big.

    Pests and problems

    • Keep after slugs; they love new transplants. West-side valleys
    • Cover carrot beds with insect netting from sowing on to keep carrot rust fly out.
    • Watch for flea beetle holes on arugula, radishes and young brassicas.

    Fruit, berries and flowers

    • Mulch strawberries and blueberries.
    • Plant dahlia tubers late in the month once the soil isn't cold and soggy. West-side valleys
    • Sow sweet peas outside early in the month. West-side valleys
