Baker City, OR frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Baker City is May 25, and the first fall frost is around September 15. Plant peas from Apr 20; plant tomatoes from May 25.

Last spring frostMay 25Typical date. Frost is over by Jun 16 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostSeptember 15Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 2 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season113 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Baker City Municipal Airport (1024 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Baker City. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
