Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland), WA frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) is April 27, and the first fall frost is around October 11. Plant peas from Mar 20; plant tomatoes from May 1.

Last spring frostApril 27Typical date. Frost is over by May 10 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostOctober 11Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 28 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season167 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Pasco Tri-Cities Airport (124 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops
