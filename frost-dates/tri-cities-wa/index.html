    How the season in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) compares

    With 167 frost-free days, Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) have the 53rd-longest season of the 86 Oregon, Washington and Idaho towns we cover. They run from 72 days in McCall to 330 days in Brookings and Gold Beach.

    The typical last frost here, April 27, is in the middle of the towns we cover in the Yakima Valley and Columbia Basin: 31 days later than Walla Walla and 22 days earlier than Goldendale.

    Spring here is steadier than in most towns: the last frost is over in 9 years out of 10 just 13 days after the typical date, against 19 for a typical town on this site.

    The season is long enough for every warm crop on our list, even pumpkins and sweet potatoes, to finish from a normal planting date.

    For a fall and winter garden, sow carrots by August 2 and set out kale plants by August 16. Fall crops here are timed to the first frost (October 11 on average) and to shortening days.

    Where these dates come from

    The frost dates for Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) come from the NOAA weather station at Pasco Tri-Cities Airport, about 2 miles north of Pasco, about 4 miles north of Kennewick and about 8 miles east of Richland, at 410 feet. It's an airport station, out on open ground, so a garden in town, on a slope or in a low spot can run a few days earlier or later.

    They're NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals: 30-year averages, not a forecast. How we pick each town's station.

    Common questions about frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)

    When is the last frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    April 27 in a typical year. Frost is over by May 10 in 9 years out of 10, Tender plants like tomatoes wait longer than that, for warm soil: see the tomato question below.

    When is the first frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    Around October 11. It comes as early as September 28 in 1 year out of 10.

    When can I plant tomatoes in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    Set out tomato plants May 1–29, after starting seeds indoors Mar 6–20. Keep frost cover handy until about May 10.

    When should I plant garlic in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    Plant garlic cloves Oct 1 – Nov 1, and harvest next summer when the lower leaves turn brown.

    How long is the growing season in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    About 167 frost-free days, shorter than most of the 86 Oregon, Washington and Idaho towns we cover.
