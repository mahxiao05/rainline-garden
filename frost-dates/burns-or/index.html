Burns, OR frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Burns is June 11, and the first fall frost is around September 6. Plant peas from Apr 30; plant tomatoes from Jun 11.

Last spring frostJune 11Typical date. Frost is over by Jun 28 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostSeptember 6Typical date. Comes as early as Aug 21 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season87 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Burns Municipal Airport (1262 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Burns. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
