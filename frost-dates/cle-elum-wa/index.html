    About Cle Elum

    Cle Elum sits on the Yakima River on the east side of Snoqualmie Pass, at about 1,900 feet. Its summers are drier and its winters colder than Seattle's.

    How the season in Cle Elum compares

    With 135 frost-free days, Cle Elum has the 90th-longest season of the 116 Oregon, Washington and Idaho towns we cover. They run from 72 days in McCall to 330 days in Brookings and Gold Beach.

    The typical last frost here, May 13, is in the middle of the towns we cover in the Yakima Valley and Columbia Basin: 47 days later than Walla Walla and 6 days earlier than Goldendale.

    The gap between the typical last frost and the date it's over in 9 years out of 10 is 19 days, about average for the towns on this site. Frost after the typical date still happens about half the time, so plan on covering warm crops until June 1.

    Even here, sweet potatoes need nearly the whole season, so plant them on time and pick shorter-season varieties when you can.

    For a fall and winter garden, sow carrots by July 17 and set out kale plants by July 31. Fall crops here are timed to the first frost (September 25 on average) and to shortening days.

    Where these dates come from

    The frost dates for Cle Elum come from NOAA's Cle Elum weather station, about 1 mile east of Cle Elum, at 1,900 feet. It's the closest weather station to Cle Elum with NOAA's 1991–2020 frost records. It's a NOAA cooperative station, not an airport station.

    They're NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals: 30-year averages, not a forecast. How we pick each town's station.

    Common questions about frost in Cle Elum

    When is the last frost in Cle Elum?

    May 13 in a typical year. Frost is over by June 1 in 9 years out of 10. Tender plants like tomatoes wait longer than that, for warm soil: see the tomato question below.

    When is the first frost in Cle Elum?

    Around September 25. It comes as early as September 11 in 1 year out of 10.

    When can I plant tomatoes in Cle Elum?

    Set out tomato plants May 20 – Jun 17, after starting seeds indoors Mar 25 – Apr 8. Keep frost cover handy until about Jun 1.

    When should I plant garlic in Cle Elum?

    Plant garlic cloves Sep 25 – Oct 31, and harvest next summer when the lower leaves turn brown.

    What growing zone is Cle Elum in?

    USDA hardiness zone 7a, on the 2023 USDA map: the coldest winter night is usually 0 to 5°F. The zone tells you which perennials, shrubs and trees survive the winter; for vegetables, go by the frost dates and planting calendar instead.

    How long is the growing season in Cle Elum?

    About 135 frost-free days, shorter than most of the 116 Oregon, Washington and Idaho towns we cover.

    How much rain does Cle Elum get?

    About 25 inches a year, counting melted snow. November is the wettest month and August the driest, with 2.1 inches from June to August (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).
