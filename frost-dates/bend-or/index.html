Bend and Redmond, OR frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Bend and Redmond is June 9, and the first fall frost is around September 14. Plant peas from Apr 28; plant tomatoes from Jun 9.

Last spring frostJune 9Typical date. Frost is over by Jun 26 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostSeptember 14Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 1 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season97 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Redmond Roberts Field (928 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Bend and Redmond. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
