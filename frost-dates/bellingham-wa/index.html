Bellingham, WA frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Bellingham is March 31, and the first fall frost is around October 30. Plant peas from Mar 1; plant tomatoes from May 10.

Last spring frostMarch 31Typical date. Frost is over by Apr 18 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostOctober 30Typical date. Comes as early as Oct 12 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season213 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Bellingham International Airport (45 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Bellingham. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
