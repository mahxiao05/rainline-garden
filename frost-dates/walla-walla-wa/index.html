    Walla Walla, WA frost dates and planting calendar

    The typical last spring frost in Walla Walla is March 27, and the first fall frost is around October 31. Plant peas from Mar 20; plant tomatoes from May 1.

    Last spring frostMar 27Typical date

    These are averages, not promises. In Walla Walla, the last frost is over by Apr 22 in 9 years out of 10, about 4 weeks after the typical date.     Common questions about gardening in Walla Walla

    When is the last frost in Walla Walla, WA?

    The typical last spring frost in Walla Walla is March 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 22. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Walla Walla Regional Airport, so any single year can be earlier or later.

    When is the first frost in Walla Walla, WA?

    The typical first fall frost in Walla Walla is October 31. In 1 year out of 10 it comes by October 15.

    How long is the growing season in Walla Walla?

    About 218 frost-free days, from the typical last spring frost to the typical first fall frost. That's the window for frost-tender crops like tomatoes, squash and beans.

    When should I plant tomatoes in Walla Walla?

    Plant tomato starts outside from May 1, once nights stay above 50°F, and start seeds indoors about six to eight weeks before that.

    What can I plant in Walla Walla for a fall harvest?

    Plant radishes, spinach, lettuce, carrots, beets, kale in mid to late summer for fall and winter harvests. Plant garlic early October to early November for next summer.
