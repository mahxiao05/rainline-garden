Spokane, WA frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Spokane is April 26, and the first fall frost is around October 12. Plant peas from Apr 1; plant tomatoes from May 20.

Last spring frostApril 26Typical date. Frost is over by May 10 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostOctober 12Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 29 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season169 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

Inland Northwest. Higher, colder interior valleys. Cold springs and an earlier fall mean a shorter season; quick-maturing varieties pay off.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Spokane International Airport (717 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Spokane. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
