    Enterprise and Joseph, OR frost dates and planting calendar

    The typical last spring frost in Enterprise and Joseph is May 24, and the first fall frost is around October 1. Plant peas from Apr 20; plant tomatoes from May 25.

    Last spring frostMay 24Typical date. Frost is over by Jul 6 in 9 years out of 10.
    First fall frostOctober 1Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 10 in 1 year out of 10.
    Frost-free season130 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

    High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

    Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Joseph (1275 m elevation).

    How the season in Enterprise and Joseph compares

    With 130 frost-free days, Enterprise and Joseph have the 62nd-longest season of the 71 Oregon and Washington towns we cover. They run from 87 days in Burns to 330 days in Brookings and Gold Beach.

    The typical last frost here, May 24, is in the middle of the towns we cover in the Columbia Gorge and eastern Oregon: 43 days later than The Dalles and 4 days earlier than John Day.

    Late frosts are harder to predict here than in most towns. There are 43 days between the typical last frost and the date it's over in 9 years out of 10, against 20 for a typical town on this site, so keep frost cover handy until July 6.

    Even here, sweet potatoes need nearly the whole season, so plant them on time and pick shorter-season varieties when you can.

    For a fall and winter garden, sow carrots by July 23 and set out kale plants by August 6. Fall crops here are timed to the first frost (October 1 on average) and to shortening days.
