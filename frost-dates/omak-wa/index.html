Omak and Okanogan, WA frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Omak and Okanogan is April 30, and the first fall frost is around October 7. Plant peas from Apr 1; plant tomatoes from May 20.

Last spring frostApril 30Typical date. Frost is over by May 15 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostOctober 7Typical date. Comes as early as Sep 23 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season160 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

Inland Northwest. Higher, colder interior valleys. Cold springs and an earlier fall mean a shorter season; quick-maturing varieties pay off.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Omak Airport (395 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops
