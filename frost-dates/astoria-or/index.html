Astoria, OR frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in Astoria is March 26, and the first fall frost is around November 11. Plant peas from Mar 25; plant tomatoes from May 20.

Last spring frostMarch 26Typical date. Frost is over by Apr 18 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostNovember 11Typical date. Comes as early as Oct 22 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season230 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Astoria Regional Airport (3 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in Astoria. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
