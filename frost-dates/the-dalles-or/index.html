The Dalles, OR frost dates and planting calendar

The typical last spring frost in The Dalles is April 11, and the first fall frost is around October 26. Plant peas from Mar 20; plant tomatoes from May 1.

Last spring frostApril 11Typical date. Frost is over by Apr 28 in 9 years out of 10.
First fall frostOctober 26Typical date. Comes as early as Oct 9 in 1 year out of 10.
Frost-free season198 daysYour growing season for frost-tender crops like tomatoes and squash.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Frost dates are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for The Dalles Municipal Airport (Dallesport, WA) (72 m elevation). Gardens in cold hollows or at higher elevation can frost later in spring and earlier in fall.

New to gardening? Start with these 5 easy crops

They're forgiving, quick and hard to kill in The Dalles. Tap one for planting dates and step-by-step instructions, or read the beginner's guide.

  • RadishesReady in about four weeks, so you see results fast.
  • LettuceGrows in cool weather and a bit of shade, and you can pick it for weeks.
  • Bush beansBig seeds that are easy to plant, and one sowing feeds you all summer.
  • Zucchini and summer squashTwo plants are plenty. It almost always produces.
  • KaleShrugs off Northwest cold and keeps going into winter.
