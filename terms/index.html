    Gardening information

    Planting dates on Rainline Garden are estimates based on 30-year weather averages and university extension guidance. Weather in any given year can be earlier or later, so check your local forecast before planting tender crops. We can't guarantee results and aren't responsible for crop losses.

    Plant safety

    Some garden plants, including several flowers and the leaves of some vegetables, are toxic to people or pets. Make sure you know what a plant is before you eat it, and check before planting where children or animals play. Follow the label on any product you use in the garden.

    Accuracy and corrections
