Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-13.

What we collect

Rainline Garden has no accounts and no forms that send data to us. The ZIP code you enter is matched to a city inside your browser and is never sent to our server. The planner remembers the last city you chose in your browser's local storage so it's ready next time; you can clear it by clearing site data in your browser.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
