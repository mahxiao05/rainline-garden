Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-14.

Rainline Garden is owned and operated by X Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the site.

What we collect

Rainline Garden has no accounts. The ZIP code you enter is matched to a city inside your browser and is never sent to our server. Your browser's local storage keeps three things on your own device: the last city you chose in the planner, your light or dark display choice, and your garden plan if you make one on the My garden plan page (the city, the plants you picked, tasks you checked off and your notes). None of it is sent to us. You can clear it by clearing site data in your browser or with the "Clear my plan" link on the plan page.

The plant question helper also runs entirely in your browser. Questions you type into it are answered from data already loaded on the page and are not sent to us or stored.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Affiliate links

Some links to products go to Amazon. Amazon may set its own cookies when you follow them.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font service.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@rainlinegarden.org.
