    Why Northwest planting dates are different

    National charts key everything off the last frost date. Here that's only half the story: the Portland airport's typical last frost is in mid-March, yet tomatoes planted then sit in cold, wet soil for two months, while the high desert can frost into June. Rainline Garden combines NOAA frost records for each area with regional timing for when the soil is actually ready. See how the dates are worked out.
