    Questions, corrections and suggestions are welcome.

    Email info@rainlinegarden.org. We read every message and usually answer within a few days.

    The most helpful notes are corrections: a frost date that doesn't match your experience, a town we should add, or advice that doesn't fit your part of the Northwest. If you write about a date, tell us your town or ZIP code so we can check it against the nearest weather station.
