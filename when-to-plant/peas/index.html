When to plant peas in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant peas from seed, directly in the garden, Mar 1 – Apr 12. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 9. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun; tolerates light shade
Spacing
2 inches apart along a trellis
Planting depth
1 inch deep
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 65 days after planting

Germinate in cool soil (about 40-50°F). Get them in early; they stall in summer heat.

How to grow peas

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, 1 inch deep, 2 inches apart along a trellis.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Pick snap and snow peas when pods are plump but still crisp, every two or three days.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Oregon Sugar Pod II (bred at OSU)
  • Sugar Snap
  • Green Arrow

Watch out for

Birds and slugs go after young shoots. Cover rows with netting until plants are a few inches tall.

What you'll need

  • Pea and bean trellis netting
  • Oregon Sugar Pod II pea seeds
