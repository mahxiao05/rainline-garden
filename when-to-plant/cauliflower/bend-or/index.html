When to plant cauliflower in Bend and Redmond, OR

Start cauliflower from seed indoors Mar 24 – Apr 7, then plant the young plants in the garden in Bend and Redmond May 12 – Jun 9.

Tricky here Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Bend and Redmond's typical last spring frost is June 9. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop can go in about 4 weeks before that.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Mar 24 – Apr 7 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 12 – Jun 9, 18 to 24 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Cut the head while it's firm and tight, before the curds start to separate. Tie the outer leaves over young heads to keep them white.

Varieties that do well here

  • Snow Crown
  • Amazing
  • Graffiti (purple)

Watch out for

A cold snap or dry spell makes plants form tiny 'button' heads. Keep the soil evenly moist and don't rush spring planting.

Fussier than broccoli: a setback from a cold snap or dry soil makes tiny 'button' heads. Fall crops are often the most reliable here.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Bend and Redmond, OR?

Typically June 9. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by June 26. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Redmond Roberts Field.

Is it too late to plant cauliflower in Bend and Redmond?

The spring planting window runs through June 9. Later plantings tend to struggle in summer heat.

When should I start cauliflower from seed indoors in Bend and Redmond?

March 24 to April 7, which is 7 to 5 weeks before the May 12 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
