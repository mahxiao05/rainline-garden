    When to plant bok choy in Oregon and Washington

    In Portland: Plant bok choy in the garden Mar 1 – Apr 5, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Jan 25 – Feb 8. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 20 – Sep 3. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 2. Find your city below.

    Updated October 16, 2026 · Dates from NOAA frost records for 30 towns. Any single year can be earlier or later. What frost dates really mean

    Difficulty
    Easy

    Frost

    Bolts as days lengthen, so get spring sowings in early. Fall crops are the easiest.

    How to grow bok choy

    1. Start seeds indoors on your city's dates in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden on your city's dates, 6 to 10 inches apart. If you plant seeds instead, sow them ¼ inch deep.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
    6. Harvest: Cut baby bok choy at about 6 inches tall, or whole heads at full size. Harvest before a flower stalk appears.

    Varieties that do well in the Northwest

    • Joi Choi
    • Toy Choy (baby size)
    • Mei Qing Choi

    Watch out for

    Flea beetles and slugs chew the leaves. Row cover and iron phosphate slug bait handle both.

    What you'll need

    • Bok choy seeds
    • Floating row cover
