When to plant sweet corn in Gresham, OR

Plant sweet corn from seed, directly in the garden in Gresham, May 10 – Jun 14.

Moderate Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Gresham's typical last spring frost is March 16. Frost kills sweet corn, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 10.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds May 10 – Jun 14, 1 inch deep, 10 inches apart in a block of short rows.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Ready about three weeks after the silks appear, when the silks are brown and the kernels squirt milky juice.

Varieties that do well here

  • Early Sunglow
  • Bodacious
  • Golden Bantam

Watch out for

Crows pull up sprouting seeds. Cover the bed with netting or row cover until plants are 4 inches tall.

Plant in blocks of at least four short rows, not one long row, for good pollination.

What you'll need

  • Early sweet corn seeds
  • Bird netting

Common questions

When is the last frost in Gresham, OR?

Typically March 16. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 12. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Portland-Troutdale Airport.

Is it too late to plant sweet corn in Gresham?

The spring planting window runs through June 14. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around November 20.

How long until I can harvest sweet corn?

About 80 days after planting. Ready about three weeks after the silks appear, when the silks are brown and the kernels squirt milky juice.
