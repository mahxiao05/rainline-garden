When to plant sweet corn in Walla Walla, WA

Plant sweet corn from seed, directly in the garden in Walla Walla, Apr 25 – May 30.

Moderate Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Walla Walla's typical last spring frost is March 27. Frost kills sweet corn, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about April 25.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds Apr 25 – May 30, 1 inch deep, 10 inches apart in a block of short rows.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Ready about three weeks after the silks appear, when the silks are brown and the kernels squirt milky juice.

Varieties that do well here

  • Early Sunglow
  • Bodacious
  • Golden Bantam

Watch out for

Crows pull up sprouting seeds. Cover the bed with netting or row cover until plants are 4 inches tall.

Plant in blocks of at least four short rows, not one long row, for good pollination.

What you'll need

  • Early sweet corn seeds
  • Bird netting

Common questions

When is the last frost in Walla Walla, WA?

Typically March 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 22. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Walla Walla Regional Airport.

Is it too late to plant sweet corn in Walla Walla?

The spring planting window runs through May 30. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 31.

How long until I can harvest sweet corn?

About 80 days after planting. Ready about three weeks after the silks appear, when the silks are brown and the kernels squirt milky juice.
