When to plant sweet corn in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant sweet corn from seed, directly in the garden, May 10 – Jun 14. In Bend: Jun 16 – Jul 21. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Moderate
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
10 inches apart in a block of short rows
Planting depth
1 inch deep
Frost
Killed by frost
First harvest
About 80 days after planting

Plant in blocks of at least four short rows, not one long row, for good pollination.

How to grow sweet corn

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, 1 inch deep, 10 inches apart in a block of short rows.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Ready about three weeks after the silks appear, when the silks are brown and the kernels squirt milky juice.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Early Sunglow
  • Bodacious
  • Golden Bantam

Watch out for

Crows pull up sprouting seeds. Cover the bed with netting or row cover until plants are 4 inches tall.

What you'll need

  • Early sweet corn seeds
  • Bird netting
