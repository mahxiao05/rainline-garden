    When to plant cilantro in Olympia, WA

    Plant cilantro from seed, directly in the garden in Olympia, Mar 18 – May 6. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 26 – Sep 9.

    Easy Plant seeds in the ground

    Add cilantro to my garden plan Get a dated to-do list and reminders for everything you grow in Olympia.

    These are averages, not promises. In Olympia, the last frost is over by May 15 in 9 years out of 10, about 2 weeks after the typical date. In fall, the first frost can come as early as Sep 28. Any single year can be earlier or later, so check the forecast before planting tender crops. What frost dates really mean

    Why these dates

    Olympia's typical last spring frost is April 29. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop can go in about 6 weeks before that.

    West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

    Step by step

    1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
    2. Plant seeds Mar 18 – May 6, ¼ inch deep, 4 inches apart.
    3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
    4. Plant again Aug 26 – Sep 9 for a fall harvest.
    5. Harvest: Cut leaves once plants are about 6 inches tall. If it bolts, let it go to seed and harvest the coriander.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Calypso (slow to bolt)
    • Santo
    • Leisure

    Watch out for

    Bolting in warm weather. Look for slow-bolt varieties and sow little and often.

    Bolts fast in summer heat. Sow a short row every three weeks in spring, and again in late summer for fall.

    What you'll need

    • Slow-bolt cilantro seeds

    When is the last frost in Olympia, WA?

    Is it too late to plant cilantro in Olympia?

    The spring planting window runs through May 6. If you miss it, plant again for fall from August 26 to September 9.

    How long until I can harvest cilantro?

    About 50 days after planting. Cut leaves once plants are about 6 inches tall. If it bolts, let it go to seed and harvest the coriander.
