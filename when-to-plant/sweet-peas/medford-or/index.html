When to plant sweet peas in Medford, OR

Plant sweet peas from seed, directly in the garden in Medford, Mar 1 – Apr 5.

Moderate Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Medford's typical last spring frost is April 8. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the west-side valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about March 1.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds Mar 1 – Apr 5, 1 inch deep (soak seeds overnight first), 4 to 6 inches apart along a trellis.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Cut stems every couple of days; the more you pick, the more they bloom. Plants stop flowering once pods form.

Varieties that do well here

  • Cupani (very fragrant)
  • Spencer mix
  • Royal Family (more heat-tolerant)

Watch out for

Aphids and powdery mildew late in the season. Sow early so plants peak before summer heat.
