When to plant bush beans in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant bush beans from seed, directly in the garden, May 10 – Jun 21. In Bend: Jun 16 – Jul 28. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
3 inches apart
Planting depth
1 inch deep
Frost
Killed by frost
First harvest
About 55 days after planting

Seeds rot in cold, wet soil. Wait until soil is consistently above 60°F.

How to grow bush beans

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, 1 inch deep, 3 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Pick when pods are pencil-thick and before the seeds bulge. Picking often keeps plants producing.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Provider (sprouts in cooler soil)
  • Blue Lake 274
  • Royal Burgundy

Watch out for

Seeds rot in cold, wet soil, the most common Northwest bean failure. Wait for warm soil.
