When to plant bush beans in Yakima, WA

Plant bush beans from seed, directly in the garden in Yakima, May 15 – Jun 26.

Easy Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Yakima's typical last spring frost is May 8. Frost kills bush beans, so planting starts once frost risk drops, around May 15. Keep row cover or a frost blanket handy for the first couple of weeks, since a late frost is still possible.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds May 15 – Jun 26, 1 inch deep, 3 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Pick when pods are pencil-thick and before the seeds bulge. Picking often keeps plants producing.

Varieties that do well here

  • Provider (sprouts in cooler soil)
  • Blue Lake 274
  • Royal Burgundy

Watch out for

Seeds rot in cold, wet soil, the most common Northwest bean failure. Wait for warm soil.

Seeds rot in cold, wet soil. Wait until soil is consistently above 60°F.
