When to plant tulips in Klamath Falls, OR

Plant tulip bulbs in Klamath Falls from Oct 15 – Nov 15. They root over winter and bloom the next spring.

Easy Plant bulbs

Why these dates

Tulips need several weeks of winter chill to bloom, so they go in during fall once the soil has cooled, ideally a few weeks before it freezes. In Klamath Falls the first fall frost usually comes around September 18.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Step by step

  1. Buy firm, heavy bulbs from a garden center or bulb grower.
  2. Pick a sunny spot with soil that drains well; bulbs rot in soggy ground.
  3. Plant them Oct 15 – Nov 15, 6 to 8 inches deep, pointed end up, 4 to 6 inches apart.
  4. Water once after planting, then let the rain take over. Leave the leaves until they yellow after blooming.
  5. Harvest: Cut when buds show color but are still closed. After blooming, let the leaves die back on their own to feed the bulb.

Varieties that do well here

  • Darwin hybrids (return best)
  • Apeldoorn
  • Queen of Night

Watch out for

Squirrels, voles and deer eat tulips. Plant bulbs in wire cages, or choose daffodils where animals are a problem.
