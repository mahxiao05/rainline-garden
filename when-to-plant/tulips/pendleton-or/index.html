When to plant tulips in Pendleton, OR

Plant tulip bulbs in Pendleton from Oct 1 – Nov 1. They root over winter and bloom the next spring.

Easy Plant bulbs

Why these dates

Tulips need several weeks of winter chill to bloom, so they go in during fall once the soil has cooled, ideally a few weeks before it freezes. In Pendleton the first fall frost usually comes around October 16.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Buy firm, heavy bulbs from a garden center or bulb grower.
  2. Pick a sunny spot with soil that drains well; bulbs rot in soggy ground.
  3. Plant them Oct 1 – Nov 1, 6 to 8 inches deep, pointed end up, 4 to 6 inches apart.
  4. Water once after planting, then let the rain take over. Leave the leaves until they yellow after blooming.
  5. Harvest: Cut when buds show color but are still closed. After blooming, let the leaves die back on their own to feed the bulb.

Varieties that do well here

  • Darwin hybrids (return best)
  • Apeldoorn
  • Queen of Night

Watch out for

Squirrels, voles and deer eat tulips. Plant bulbs in wire cages, or choose daffodils where animals are a problem.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Pendleton, OR?

Typically April 26. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Pendleton Airport.

When do tulips bloom in Pendleton?

In spring, usually April and May, depending on the variety and the year. Cut when buds show color but are still closed. After blooming, let the leaves die back on their own to feed the bulb.
