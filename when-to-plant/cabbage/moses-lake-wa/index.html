When to plant cabbage in Moses Lake, WA

Start cabbage from seed indoors Feb 11–25, then plant the young plants in the garden in Moses Lake Apr 1 – May 6. For a fall harvest, plant again Jul 16–30.

Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Moses Lake's typical last spring frost is April 27. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 1.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Feb 11–25 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden Apr 1 – May 6, 18 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Plant again Jul 16–30 for a fall harvest.
  6. Harvest: Cut when heads feel firm when squeezed. Water evenly to keep heads from splitting.

Varieties that do well here

  • Golden Acre
  • Early Jersey Wakefield
  • January King (winter)

Watch out for

Cabbage worms and root maggots. Row cover from planting day prevents both.

Even watering prevents split heads.

What you'll need

  • Cabbage seeds
  • Floating row cover

Common questions

When is the last frost in Moses Lake, WA?

Typically April 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 11. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Moses Lake Grant County Airport.

Is it too late to plant cabbage in Moses Lake?

The spring planting window runs through May 6. If you miss it, plant again for fall from July 16 to July 30.

When should I start cabbage from seed indoors in Moses Lake?

February 11 to February 25, which is 7 to 5 weeks before the April 1 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
