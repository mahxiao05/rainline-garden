When to plant cabbage in Burns, OR

Start cabbage from seed indoors Mar 26 – Apr 9, then plant the young plants in the garden in Burns May 14 – Jun 18.

Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Burns's typical last spring frost is June 11. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop can go in about 4 weeks before that.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Mar 26 – Apr 9 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 14 – Jun 18, 18 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Cut when heads feel firm when squeezed. Water evenly to keep heads from splitting.

Varieties that do well here

  • Golden Acre
  • Early Jersey Wakefield
  • January King (winter)

Watch out for

Cabbage worms and root maggots. Row cover from planting day prevents both.

Even watering prevents split heads.

What you'll need

  • Cabbage seeds
  • Floating row cover

Common questions

When is the last frost in Burns, OR?

Typically June 11. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by June 28. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Burns Municipal Airport.

Is it too late to plant cabbage in Burns?

The spring planting window runs through June 18. Later plantings tend to struggle in summer heat.

When should I start cabbage from seed indoors in Burns?

March 26 to April 9, which is 7 to 5 weeks before the May 14 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
