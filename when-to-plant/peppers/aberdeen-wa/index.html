    When to plant peppers in Aberdeen and Hoquiam, WA

    Start peppers from seed indoors Mar 11–25, then plant the young plants in the garden in Aberdeen and Hoquiam May 20 – Jun 10.

    Tricky here Plant young plants (starts)

    Why these dates

    Aberdeen and Hoquiam's typical last spring frost is March 30. Frost kills peppers, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

    Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

    Difficulty
    Tricky here
    Sun
    Full sun; against a warm wall if possible
    Spacing
    18 inches apart
    Planting depth
    Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot
    Frost
    Killed by frost
    First harvest
    About 75 days after planting

    Step by step

    1. Start seeds indoors Mar 11–25 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 10, 18 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Harvest: Pick green any time once full size, or leave them to ripen red, which can take three more weeks.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Gypsy
    • Ace
    • Early Jalapeño
    • Lunchbox snacking peppers

    Watch out for

    Cool nights stall growth. Black plastic mulch and row cover early in the season make a big difference here.

    Even more heat-hungry than tomatoes. A warm wall or black plastic mulch helps.

    What you'll need

    • Pepper seeds for short seasons
    • Black plastic mulch
    • Seedling heat mat

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Aberdeen and Hoquiam, WA?

    Typically March 30. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 25. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Hoquiam Bowerman Airport.

    Is it too late to plant peppers in Aberdeen and Hoquiam?

    The spring planting window runs through June 10. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around November 14.

    When should I start peppers from seed indoors in Aberdeen and Hoquiam?

    March 11 to March 25, which is 10 to 8 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
