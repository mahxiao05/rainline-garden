When to plant pole beans in Aberdeen and Hoquiam, WA

Plant pole beans from seed, directly in the garden in Aberdeen and Hoquiam, May 15 – Jun 19.

Easy Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Aberdeen and Hoquiam's typical last spring frost is March 30. Frost kills pole beans, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 15.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds May 15 – Jun 19, 1 inch deep, 4 to 6 inches apart along a trellis.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Harvest: Pick when pods are pencil-thick and before the seeds bulge. Picking every few days keeps the vines producing.

Watch out for

Seeds rot in cold, wet soil, the most common Northwest bean failure. Wait for warm soil.

Is it too late to plant pole beans in Aberdeen and Hoquiam?

The spring planting window runs through June 19. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around November 14.
