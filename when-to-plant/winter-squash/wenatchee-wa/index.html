When to plant winter squash and pumpkins in Wenatchee, WA

Plant winter squash and pumpkins in the garden in Wenatchee May 1–22, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 3–10.

Moderate Plant seeds or young plants

Why these dates

Wenatchee's typical last spring frost is April 8. Frost kills winter squash and pumpkins, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 1.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 3–10 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 1–22, 4 feet apart (vines spread). If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Cut when the rind resists a fingernail and the stem is dry, before a hard frost. Cure in a warm room for two weeks.

Varieties that do well here

  • Delicata
  • Sugar Pie pumpkin
  • Sweet Meat (an Oregon heirloom)

Watch out for

A short or cool season. Delicata and other fast varieties ripen far more reliably here than big pumpkins.

Long season. Harvest before hard frost and cure in a warm spot for storage.

What you'll need

  • Delicata squash seeds
  • Black plastic mulch

Common questions

When is the last frost in Wenatchee, WA?

Typically April 8. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 23. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Wenatchee Pangborn Airport.

Is it too late to plant winter squash and pumpkins in Wenatchee?

The spring planting window runs through May 22. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 24.

When should I start winter squash and pumpkins from seed indoors in Wenatchee?

April 3 to April 10, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 1 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
