When to plant winter squash and pumpkins in Wilsonville and Canby, OR

Plant winter squash and pumpkins in the garden in Wilsonville and Canby May 10–31, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 12–19.

Moderate Plant seeds or young plants

Why these dates

Wilsonville and Canby's typical last spring frost is March 16. Frost kills winter squash and pumpkins, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 10.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 12–19 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 10–31, 4 feet apart (vines spread). If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Cut when the rind resists a fingernail and the stem is dry, before a hard frost. Cure in a warm room for two weeks.

Varieties that do well here

  • Delicata
  • Sugar Pie pumpkin
  • Sweet Meat (an Oregon heirloom)

Watch out for

A short or cool season. Delicata and other fast varieties ripen far more reliably here than big pumpkins.

Long season. Harvest before hard frost and cure in a warm spot for storage.

What you'll need

  • Delicata squash seeds
  • Black plastic mulch

Common questions

When is the last frost in Wilsonville and Canby, OR?

Typically March 16. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 7. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Aurora State Airport.

Is it too late to plant winter squash and pumpkins in Wilsonville and Canby?

The spring planting window runs through May 31. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around November 14.

When should I start winter squash and pumpkins from seed indoors in Wilsonville and Canby?

April 12 to April 19, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 10 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
