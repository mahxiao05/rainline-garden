    When to plant eggplant in Burns, OR

    Start eggplant from seed indoors Apr 2–16, then plant the young plants in the garden in Burns Jun 11 – Jul 2.

    Tricky here Plant young plants (starts)

    Why these dates

    Burns's typical last spring frost is June 11. Frost kills eggplant, so planting starts once frost risk drops, around June 11. Keep row cover or a frost blanket handy for the first couple of weeks, since a late frost is still possible. The season is a little tight here, so an early variety is the safer choice.

    High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

    Difficulty
    Tricky here
    Sun
    Full sun; the hottest spot you have
    Spacing
    18 to 24 inches apart
    Planting depth
    Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot
    Frost
    Killed by frost
    First harvest
    About 80 days after planting

    Step by step

    1. Start seeds indoors Apr 2–16 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden Jun 11 – Jul 2, 18 to 24 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Harvest: Pick when the skin is glossy and springs back when pressed. Dull skin means it's overripe and seedy.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Hansel (early, small fruit)
    • Fairy Tale
    • Orient Express

    Watch out for

    Flea beetles riddle young leaves, and cool nights stall growth. Keep row cover on until plants are growing strongly.

    Needs even more heat than peppers. Small-fruited, early varieties do best west of the Cascades, helped by black plastic mulch or a warm wall. Hot eastern valleys grow them easily.

    What you'll need

    • Eggplant seeds
    • Black plastic mulch

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Burns, OR?

    Typically June 11. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by June 28. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Burns Municipal Airport.

    Is it too late to plant eggplant in Burns?

    The spring planting window runs through July 2. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around September 6.

    When should I start eggplant from seed indoors in Burns?

    April 2 to April 16, which is 10 to 8 weeks before the June 11 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
