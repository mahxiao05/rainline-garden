    Why these dates

    Long Beach and Ilwaco's typical last spring frost is April 6. Frost kills eggplant, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

    Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

    How to grow eggplant in Long Beach and Ilwaco

    1. Start seeds indoors Mar 11–25 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 10, 18 to 24 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Harvest: Pick when the skin is glossy and springs back when pressed. Dull skin means it's overripe and seedy.

    Growing notes

    What makes the difference in Northwest gardens, in plain language.

    Soil, warmth and feeding

    Eggplant loves heat even more than peppers. Give it well-drained soil rich in compost with a pH of about 5.8 to 6.8, and wait to plant until nights stay above about 55°F and the soil has warmed. Plants set out into cold soil stall and often never recover their pace. Feed with a balanced fertilizer at planting and lightly again when fruit sets. Water deeply and evenly, about an inch a week.

    Growing it east vs west of the Cascades

    West of the Cascades, eggplant is a challenge because summers are mild and nights are cool. Choose small-fruited, early varieties like Hansel, Fairy Tale and Orient Express, plant them in your hottest spot, and use black plastic mulch and row cover early in the season. Growing in a large dark container against a south wall also works well. East of the Cascades, hot valleys grow eggplant easily. Flowers can drop in extreme heat, though, so steady water and mulch help there.

    Flea beetles, wilt and harvest

    Flea beetles chew many tiny holes in young leaves and can stunt small plants. Keep row cover on until plants grow strongly, then remove it so bees can reach the flowers. Verticillium wilt, a soil disease shared with tomatoes and potatoes, causes leaves to yellow and wilt on one side; rotate so eggplant doesn't follow those crops. Pick fruit when the skin is glossy and springs back when pressed. Eggplant doesn't store long: keep it a few days in a cool spot, since the fridge can cause brown spots and pitting.

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Long Beach and Ilwaco, WA?

    Typically April 6. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 28. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Long Beach.

    Is it too late to plant eggplant in Long Beach and Ilwaco?

    The spring planting window runs through June 10. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 26.

    When should I start eggplant from seed indoors in Long Beach and Ilwaco?

    March 11 to March 25, which is 10 to 8 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.

    Why isn't my eggplant producing fruit?

    Cool nights are the most common cause in the Northwest, and extreme heat can do the same in eastern valleys. Plants also need pollinators, so remove row cover once flowers open. Small, early varieties set fruit most reliably here.

    What are the tiny holes in my eggplant leaves?

    Flea beetles. They're worst on young plants in spring. Use row cover until plants are growing strongly, and older plants usually outgrow the damage.

    How do I know when eggplant is ripe?

    Look for glossy skin that springs back when pressed gently. Dull skin and brown seeds mean it's overripe. Cut the stem with pruners rather than pulling, since the stems are tough and sometimes spiny.
