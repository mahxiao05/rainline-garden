When to plant cucumbers in Spokane, WA

Plant cucumbers in the garden in Spokane May 20 – Jun 17, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 22–29.

Moderate Plant seeds or young plants

Why these dates

Spokane's typical last spring frost is April 26. Frost kills cucumbers, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

Inland Northwest. Higher, colder interior valleys. Cold springs and an earlier fall mean a shorter season; quick-maturing varieties pay off.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 22–29 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 17, 12 inches apart on a trellis. If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick slicers at 6 to 8 inches, before they yellow. Check plants every day or two in midsummer.

Varieties that do well here

  • Marketmore 76
  • Lemon
  • Diva

Watch out for

Powdery mildew in late summer. Grow them up a trellis for airflow and water at the base.

Need warm soil and warm nights. Floating row cover helps in cool springs.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Spokane, WA?

Typically April 26. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Spokane International Airport.

Is it too late to plant cucumbers in Spokane?

The spring planting window runs through June 17. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 12.

When should I start cucumbers from seed indoors in Spokane?

April 22 to April 29, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
