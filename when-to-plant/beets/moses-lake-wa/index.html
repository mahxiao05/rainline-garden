When to plant beets in Moses Lake, WA

Plant beets from seed, directly in the garden in Moses Lake, Apr 1 – May 27. For a fall harvest, plant again Jul 30 – Aug 13.

Moderate Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Moses Lake's typical last spring frost is April 27. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 1.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Difficulty
Moderate

First harvest
About 60 days after planting

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds Apr 1 – May 27, ½ inch deep, Thin to 3 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again Jul 30 – Aug 13 for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Pull at golf ball to tennis ball size. The greens are edible too.

Varieties that do well here

  • Detroit Dark Red
  • Chioggia
  • Early Wonder

Watch out for

Leaf miners leave pale trails in the leaves. Pick off affected leaves and use row cover.

Each seed cluster sprouts several seedlings; thin to 3 inches apart.

What you'll need

  • Detroit Dark Red beet seeds
  • Floating row cover

Common questions

When is the last frost in Moses Lake, WA?

Typically April 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 11. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Moses Lake Grant County Airport.

Is it too late to plant beets in Moses Lake?

The spring planting window runs through May 27. If you miss it, plant again for fall from July 30 to August 13.

How long until I can harvest beets?

About 60 days after planting. Pull at golf ball to tennis ball size. The greens are edible too.
