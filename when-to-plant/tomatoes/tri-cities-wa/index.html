When to plant tomatoes in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland), WA

Start tomatoes from seed indoors Mar 6–20, then plant the young plants in the garden in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) May 1–29.

Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)'s typical last spring frost is April 27. Frost kills tomatoes, so planting starts once frost risk drops, around May 1. Keep row cover or a frost blanket handy for the first couple of weeks, since a late frost is still possible.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Mar 6–20 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 1–29, 2 feet apart. Bury the stem up to the first leaves.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick when fully colored. Before fall rains, pick green tomatoes and ripen them indoors.

Varieties that do well here

  • Stupice
  • Early Girl
  • Sungold (cherry)
  • Oregon Spring (bred in Oregon)

Watch out for

Late blight in wet falls turns leaves and fruit brown. Keep leaves dry and give plants space.

Wait for warm soil and mild nights before planting out. Early or cherry varieties ripen most reliably here.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland), WA?

Typically April 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Pasco Tri-Cities Airport.

Is it too late to plant tomatoes in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

The spring planting window runs through May 29. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 11.

When should I start tomatoes from seed indoors in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

March 6 to March 20, which is 8 to 6 weeks before the May 1 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
