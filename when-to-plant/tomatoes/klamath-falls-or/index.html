When to plant tomatoes in Klamath Falls, OR

Start tomatoes from seed indoors Apr 14–28, then plant the young plants in the garden in Klamath Falls Jun 9 – Jul 7.

Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Klamath Falls's typical last spring frost is June 9. Frost kills tomatoes, so planting starts once frost risk drops, around June 9. Keep row cover or a frost blanket handy for the first couple of weeks, since a late frost is still possible.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 14–28 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden Jun 9 – Jul 7, 2 feet apart. Bury the stem up to the first leaves.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick when fully colored. Before fall rains, pick green tomatoes and ripen them indoors.

Varieties that do well here

  • Stupice
  • Early Girl
  • Sungold (cherry)
  • Oregon Spring (bred at OSU)

Watch out for

Late blight in wet falls turns leaves and fruit brown. Keep leaves dry and give plants space.

OSU Extension advises waiting for soil consistently above 70°F. Early or cherry varieties ripen most reliably here.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Klamath Falls, OR?

Typically June 9. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by June 26. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Klamath Falls Airport.

Is it too late to plant tomatoes in Klamath Falls?

The spring planting window runs through July 7. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around September 18.

When should I start tomatoes from seed indoors in Klamath Falls?

April 14 to April 28, which is 8 to 6 weeks before the June 9 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
