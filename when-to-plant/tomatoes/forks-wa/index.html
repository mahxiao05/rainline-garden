When to plant tomatoes in Forks, WA

Start tomatoes from seed indoors Mar 25 – Apr 8, then plant the young plants in the garden in Forks May 20 – Jun 17.

Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

Why these dates

Forks's typical last spring frost is April 20. Frost kills tomatoes, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Mar 25 – Apr 8 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 17, 2 feet apart. Bury the stem up to the first leaves.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick when fully colored. Before fall rains, pick green tomatoes and ripen them indoors.

Varieties that do well here

  • Stupice
  • Early Girl
  • Sungold (cherry)
  • Oregon Spring (bred in Oregon)

Watch out for

Late blight in wet falls turns leaves and fruit brown. Keep leaves dry and give plants space.

Wait for warm soil and mild nights before planting out. Early or cherry varieties ripen most reliably here.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Forks, WA?

Typically April 20. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Quillayute Airport.

Is it too late to plant tomatoes in Forks?

The spring planting window runs through June 17. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 26.

When should I start tomatoes from seed indoors in Forks?

March 25 to April 8, which is 8 to 6 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
