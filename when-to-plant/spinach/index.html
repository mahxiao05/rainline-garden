When to plant spinach in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant spinach from seed, directly in the garden, Mar 1 – Apr 5. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 20 – Sep 10. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 2. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Moderate
Sun
Full sun to part shade
Spacing
3 inches apart
Planting depth
½ inch deep
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 45 days after planting

Bolts when days get long and warm, so spring sowings should go in early.

How to grow spinach

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, ½ inch deep, 3 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Cut outer leaves once plants have five or six, or cut the whole plant before it sends up a flower stalk.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Bloomsdale Long Standing
  • Space
  • Giant Winter (for fall sowing)

Watch out for

Bolts (goes to seed) as days lengthen in late spring. Sow early, and sow again in late summer for fall and winter.

What you'll need

  • Bloomsdale spinach seeds
  • Floating row cover
