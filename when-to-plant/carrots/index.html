When to plant carrots in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant carrots from seed, directly in the garden, Mar 15 – May 10. For a fall harvest, plant again Jul 23 – Aug 6. In Bend: May 12 – Jul 7. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Moderate
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
Thin to 2 inches apart
Planting depth
¼ inch deep
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 70 days after planting

Keep the seedbed moist until they sprout, which can take two to three weeks in cool soil.

How to grow carrots

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, ¼ inch deep, Thin to 2 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Pull when the shoulders are about ¾ inch across. Fall carrots sweeten after a light frost.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Scarlet Nantes
  • Bolero
  • Danvers

Watch out for

Carrot rust fly larvae tunnel through roots. Cover the bed with insect netting from sowing on.

What you'll need

  • Scarlet Nantes carrot seeds
  • Insect netting
