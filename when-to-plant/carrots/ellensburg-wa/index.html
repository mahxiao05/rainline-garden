When to plant carrots in Ellensburg, WA

Plant carrots from seed, directly in the garden in Ellensburg, Apr 15 – Jun 10. For a fall harvest, plant again Jul 15–29.

Moderate Plant seeds in the ground

Why these dates

Ellensburg's typical last spring frost is April 28. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Inland Northwest, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 15.

Inland Northwest. Higher, colder interior valleys. Cold springs and an earlier fall mean a shorter season; quick-maturing varieties pay off.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds Apr 15 – Jun 10, ¼ inch deep, Thin to 2 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again Jul 15–29 for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Pull when the shoulders are about ¾ inch across. Fall carrots sweeten after a light frost.

Varieties that do well here

  • Scarlet Nantes
  • Bolero
  • Danvers

Watch out for

Carrot rust fly larvae tunnel through roots. Cover the bed with insect netting from sowing on.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Ellensburg, WA?

Typically April 28. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 12. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Ellensburg Bowers Field.

Is it too late to plant carrots in Ellensburg?

The spring planting window runs through June 10. If you miss it, plant again for fall from July 15 to July 29.

How long until I can harvest carrots?

About 70 days after planting. Pull when the shoulders are about ¾ inch across. Fall carrots sweeten after a light frost.
