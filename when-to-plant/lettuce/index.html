When to plant lettuce in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant lettuce in the garden Mar 1 – Apr 26, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Jan 18 – Feb 1. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 20 – Sep 3. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 23. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun in spring; afternoon shade in summer
Spacing
8 to 10 inches apart for heads
Planting depth
¼ inch deep (it needs light to sprout)
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 50 days after planting

Tolerates light frost. Sow every two to three weeks; choose heat-tolerant types for later sowings.

How to grow lettuce

  1. Start seeds indoors on your city's dates in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden on your city's dates, 8 to 10 inches apart for heads. If you plant seeds instead, sow them ¼ inch deep (it needs light to sprout).
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
  6. Harvest: Pick outer leaves as needed, or cut whole heads when full. Harvest in the morning for the crispest leaves.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Buttercrunch
  • Black Seeded Simpson
  • Winter Density

Watch out for

Slugs are the Northwest lettuce pest. Iron phosphate slug bait is safe around pets and edible crops.
