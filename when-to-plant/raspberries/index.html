When to plant raspberries in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant raspberry canes Mar 15 – Apr 26. In Bend: May 12 – Jun 23. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Moderate
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
2 to 3 feet apart in rows
Planting depth
Roots covered by 2 to 3 inches of soil
Frost
Hardy perennial; comes back every year
First harvest
Light crop in year two (fall-bearers can fruit the first fall)

Need well-drained soil; they die in soggy ground. A raised row helps in wet Northwest winters. West of the Cascades, fall planting works well too.

How to grow raspberries

  1. Pick a sunny spot with soil that drains well, and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant raspberry canes on your city's dates, 2 to 3 feet apart in rows. Roots covered by 2 to 3 inches of soil.
  3. Set up a simple trellis with posts and wire. After harvest, cut spent canes to the ground.
  4. Harvest: Pick when berries slip easily off the core. Check every two or three days in season.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Meeker (a Northwest favorite)
  • Tulameen
  • Heritage (fall-bearing)
  • Cascade Delight

Watch out for

Root rot in soggy soil. Plant in a raised row or a bed that drains well.

What you'll need

  • Raspberry canes (bare root)
  • Garden trellis wire and posts
  • Pruning shears
