When to plant strawberries in Burns, OR

Plant strawberry plants in Burns May 14 – Jun 25.

Easy Plant young plants

Why these dates

Burns's typical last spring frost is June 11. As a hardy perennial, this crop can go in about 4 weeks before that.

High desert and mountains. Central and high eastern Oregon. Frost is possible almost any month. Use row covers, wall-o-water or cloches for warm crops, and choose short-season varieties.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
12 to 18 inches apart

Frost
Hardy perennial; comes back every year
First harvest
A light crop the first summer; full crops from the second year

Step by step

  1. Pick a sunny spot with soil that drains well, and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant strawberry plants May 14 – Jun 25, 12 to 18 inches apart. Crown at soil level: not buried, roots covered.
  3. Water regularly, mulch with straw, and pinch off runners in the first year so plants put energy into roots.
  4. Harvest: Pick fully red berries every couple of days, in the morning, with the cap attached.

Varieties that do well here

  • Hood (an Oregon classic)
  • Shuksan
  • Seascape (everbearing)
  • Albion (day-neutral)

Watch out for

Gray mold (botrytis) in wet springs and slugs on ripe berries. Mulch with straw to keep fruit off the soil.

June-bearers give one big crop; everbearers and day-neutrals give smaller harvests through summer. Oregon and Washington breeding programs have produced many of the best varieties for this climate.

What you'll need

  • Strawberry plants (bare root)
  • Straw mulch
  • Bird netting

Common questions

When is the last frost in Burns, OR?

Typically June 11. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by June 28. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Burns Municipal Airport.

Is it too late to plant strawberries in Burns?

The spring planting window runs through June 25. Potted plants can still go in later if you water them faithfully through their first summer.

How long until I can pick strawberries?

A light crop the first summer; full crops from the second year. Pick fully red berries every couple of days, in the morning, with the cap attached.
