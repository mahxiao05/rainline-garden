When to plant dahlias in Bellingham, WA

Plant dahlia tubers in Bellingham May 10 – Jun 7.

Moderate Plant tubers

Why these dates

Bellingham's typical last spring frost is March 31. Frost kills dahlias, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 10.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Step by step

  1. Pick a sunny spot with soil that drains well, and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant dahlia tubers May 10 – Jun 7, 18 to 24 inches apart. 4 to 6 inches deep, eye pointing up.
  3. Don't water tubers until shoots appear, then water deeply once a week. Stake tall varieties when you plant so you don't spear the tuber later.
  4. Harvest: Cut flowers when they're almost fully open, early in the morning. The more you cut, the more they bloom.

Varieties that do well here

  • Café au Lait
  • Bishop of Llandaff
  • Cornel Brons

Watch out for

Slugs and earwigs chew young shoots and petals. Use iron phosphate slug bait early in the season.

Tubers rot in cold, wet soil, so wait until it warms. West of the Cascades many gardeners leave them in the ground over winter under thick mulch; east of the mountains, dig and store them after the first frost.

What you'll need

  • Dahlia tubers
  • Plant stakes
  • Iron phosphate slug bait

Common questions

When is the last frost in Bellingham, WA?

Typically March 31. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 18. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Bellingham International Airport.

Is it too late to plant dahlias in Bellingham?

The spring planting window runs through June 7. Later plantings may not have time to bloom well before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 30.

When do dahlias bloom?

Blooms from midsummer until the first frost. Cut flowers when they're almost fully open, early in the morning. The more you cut, the more they bloom.
