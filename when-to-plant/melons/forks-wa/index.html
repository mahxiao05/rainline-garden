When to plant melons in Forks, WA

Plant melons in the garden in Forks May 20 – Jun 10, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 22–29.

Tricky here Plant seeds or young plants

Why these dates

Forks's typical last spring frost is April 20. Frost kills melons, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 22–29 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 10, 24 to 36 inches apart. If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Cantaloupes are ripe when the stem slips off with a gentle push. Watermelons are ready when the curly tendril nearest the fruit turns brown and dry.

Varieties that do well here

  • Sugar Baby (watermelon)
  • Minnesota Midget (cantaloupe)
  • Halona (cantaloupe)

Watch out for

Cool soil and cool nights are the main obstacle west of the Cascades. Black plastic mulch and row cover until flowering make the difference.

The toughest heat lover for west-side gardens: choose short-season varieties and use black plastic and row cover. Eastern valleys grow them easily.

What you'll need

  • Black plastic mulch

Common questions

When is the last frost in Forks, WA?

Typically April 20. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Quillayute Airport.

Is it too late to plant melons in Forks?

The spring planting window runs through June 10. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 26.

When should I start melons from seed indoors in Forks?

April 22 to April 29, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
