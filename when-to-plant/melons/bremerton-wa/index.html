    When to plant melons in Bremerton and Port Orchard, WA

    Plant melons in the garden in Bremerton and Port Orchard May 10–31, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 12–19.

    Tricky here Plant seeds or young plants

    Why these dates

    Bremerton and Port Orchard's typical last spring frost is March 29. Frost kills melons, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 10.

    West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

    Difficulty
    Tricky here
    Sun
    Full sun; the hottest spot you have
    Spacing
    24 to 36 inches apart
    Planting depth
    1 inch deep
    Frost
    Killed by frost
    First harvest
    About 85 days after planting

    Step by step

    1. Start seeds indoors Apr 12–19 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden May 10–31, 24 to 36 inches apart. If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Harvest: Cantaloupes are ripe when the stem slips off with a gentle push. Watermelons are ready when the curly tendril nearest the fruit turns brown and dry.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Sugar Baby (watermelon)
    • Minnesota Midget (cantaloupe)
    • Halona (cantaloupe)

    Watch out for

    Cool soil and cool nights are the main obstacle west of the Cascades. Black plastic mulch and row cover until flowering make the difference.

    The toughest heat lover for west-side gardens: choose short-season varieties and use black plastic and row cover. Eastern valleys grow them easily.

    What you'll need

    • Black plastic mulch
    • Floating row cover

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Bremerton and Port Orchard, WA?

    Typically March 29. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 24. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Bremerton.

    Is it too late to plant melons in Bremerton and Port Orchard?

    The spring planting window runs through May 31. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around November 14.

    When should I start melons from seed indoors in Bremerton and Port Orchard?

    April 12 to April 19, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 10 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
