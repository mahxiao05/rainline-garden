    When to plant broccoli in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland), WA

    Start broccoli from seed indoors Feb 11–25, then plant the young plants in the garden in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland) Apr 1 – May 6. For a fall harvest, plant again Jul 19 – Aug 2.

    Moderate Plant young plants (starts)

    Why these dates

    Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)'s typical last spring frost is April 27. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 1.

    Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

    Step by step

    1. Start seeds indoors Feb 11–25 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
    2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
    3. Plant them in the garden Apr 1 – May 6, 18 inches apart. Plant starts at the depth they grew in the pot.
    4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
    5. Plant again Jul 19 – Aug 2 for a fall harvest.
    6. Harvest: Cut the main head while the buds are tight and dark green. Side shoots follow for weeks.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Packman
    • Green Magic
    • Purple Sprouting (overwinters west of the Cascades)

    Watch out for

    Cabbage worms and aphids. Cover young plants with insect netting.

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland), WA?

    Typically April 27. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Pasco Tri-Cities Airport.

    Is it too late to plant broccoli in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    The spring planting window runs through May 6. If you miss it, plant again for fall from July 19 to August 2.

    When should I start broccoli from seed indoors in Tri-Cities (Pasco, Kennewick, Richland)?

    February 11 to February 25, which is 7 to 5 weeks before the April 1 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
