When to plant zucchini and summer squash in Forks, WA

Plant zucchini and summer squash in the garden in Forks May 20 – Jun 17, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 22–29.

Easy Plant seeds or young plants

Why these dates

Forks's typical last spring frost is April 20. Frost kills zucchini and summer squash, and warm-season crops also need warm soil. Soil here stays cold for weeks after the last frost, so planting waits until about May 20.

Coast. Mild winters but cool, foggy summers. Frost is rarely the problem; warmth is. Pick early, cool-tolerant varieties of tomatoes and squash.

Step by step

  1. Start seeds indoors Apr 22–29 in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden May 20 – Jun 17, 3 feet apart. If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick zucchini at 6 to 8 inches. Miss a few days and you'll have a baseball bat.

Varieties that do well here

  • Black Beauty
  • Costata Romanesco
  • Early Prolific Straightneck

Watch out for

Powdery mildew late in the season. Remove the worst leaves and keep watering at the base.

Two plants feed most families. Pick fruit small and often.

Common questions

When is the last frost in Forks, WA?

Typically April 20. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 10. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Quillayute Airport.

Is it too late to plant zucchini and summer squash in Forks?

The spring planting window runs through June 17. Later plantings may not have time to produce before the first fall frost, which usually arrives around October 26.

When should I start zucchini and summer squash from seed indoors in Forks?

April 22 to April 29, which is 4 to 3 weeks before the May 20 planting date. Or buy young plants at a garden center instead.
