When to plant zucchini and summer squash in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant zucchini and summer squash in the garden May 10 – Jun 7, either as seeds or as young plants started indoors Apr 12–19. In Bend: Jun 9 – Jul 7. Find your city below.

How to grow zucchini and summer squash

  1. Start seeds indoors on your city's dates in trays or small pots under a grow light or in your sunniest window. Or skip this step and buy young plants (starts) at a garden center.
  2. Harden off the young plants: about a week before planting, set them outside for a few hours a day, a little longer each day.
  3. Plant them in the garden on your city's dates, 3 feet apart. If you plant seeds instead, sow them 1 inch deep.
  4. Water deeply once or twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch once the soil is warm.
  5. Harvest: Pick zucchini at 6 to 8 inches. Miss a few days and you'll have a baseball bat.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Black Beauty
  • Costata Romanesco
  • Early Prolific Straightneck

Watch out for

Powdery mildew late in the season. Remove the worst leaves and keep watering at the base.

What you'll need

  • Zucchini seeds
