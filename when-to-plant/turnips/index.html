When to plant turnips in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant turnips from seed, directly in the garden, Mar 1 – Apr 12. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 13–27. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 9. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun
Spacing
Thin to 4 inches apart
Planting depth
½ inch deep
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 50 days after planting

Harvest small for tender roots. Fall turnips sweeten after a light frost.

How to grow turnips

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, ½ inch deep, Thin to 4 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Pull at 2 to 3 inches across for tender roots. The greens are good to eat too.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Hakurei (salad turnip)
  • Purple Top White Globe
  • Scarlet Queen

Watch out for

Cabbage root maggots tunnel into roots in cool, wet soil. Cover the bed with row cover from sowing on.

What you'll need

  • Hakurei turnip seeds
  • Floating row cover
