    When to plant turnips in Ellensburg, WA

    Plant turnips from seed, directly in the garden in Ellensburg, Apr 1 – May 13. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 5–19.

    Easy Plant seeds in the ground

    Add turnips to my garden plan Get a dated to-do list and reminders for everything you grow in Ellensburg.

    These are averages, not promises. In Ellensburg, the last frost is over by May 12 in 9 years out of 10, about 2 weeks after the typical date. In fall, the first frost can come as early as Sep 26. Any single year can be earlier or later, so check the forecast before planting tender crops. What frost dates really mean

    Why these dates

    Ellensburg's typical last spring frost is April 28. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Inland Northwest, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 1.

    Inland Northwest. Higher, colder interior valleys. Cold springs and an earlier fall mean a shorter season; quick-maturing varieties pay off.

    Step by step

    1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
    2. Plant seeds Apr 1 – May 13, ½ inch deep, Thin to 4 inches apart.
    3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
    4. Plant again Aug 5–19 for a fall harvest.
    5. Harvest: Pull at 2 to 3 inches across for tender roots. The greens are good to eat too.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Hakurei (salad turnip)
    • Purple Top White Globe
    • Scarlet Queen

    Watch out for

    Cabbage root maggots tunnel into roots in cool, wet soil. Cover the bed with row cover from sowing on.

    Harvest small for tender roots. Fall turnips sweeten after a light frost.

    What you'll need

    • Hakurei turnip seeds
    • Floating row cover

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Ellensburg, WA?

    Typically April 28. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by May 12. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Ellensburg Bowers Field.

    Is it too late to plant turnips in Ellensburg?

    The spring planting window runs through May 13. If you miss it, plant again for fall from August 5 to August 19.

    How long until I can harvest turnips?

    About 50 days after planting. Pull at 2 to 3 inches across for tender roots. The greens are good to eat too.
