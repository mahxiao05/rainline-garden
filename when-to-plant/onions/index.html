When to plant onions in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant onion sets Mar 1 – Apr 5. In Bend: Apr 28 – Jun 2. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Moderate
Sun
Full sun

4 inches apart

Sets 1 inch deep, tip just showing
Frost

About 110 days after planting

Plant as soon as the soil is dry enough to work. Pick long-day varieties for the Northwest.

How to grow onions

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant onion sets on your city's dates, 4 inches apart, sets 1 inch deep, tip just showing.
  3. Water regularly and keep the bed weeded; onions don't compete well.
  4. Harvest: When the tops flop over in late summer, pull the onions and let them dry in a shady, airy spot for two weeks.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Walla Walla Sweet
  • Copra (stores well)
  • Redwing

Watch out for

Weeds outcompete onions' thin leaves. Keep the bed weeded and mulched.
