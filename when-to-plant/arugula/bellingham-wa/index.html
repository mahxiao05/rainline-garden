    When to plant arugula in Bellingham, WA

    Plant arugula from seed, directly in the garden in Bellingham, Mar 1 – Apr 12. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 27 – Sep 17.

    Easy Plant seeds in the ground

    These are averages, not promises. In Bellingham, the last frost is over by Apr 18 in 9 years out of 10, about 3 weeks after the typical date. In fall, the first frost can come as early as Oct 12. Any single year can be earlier or later, so check the forecast before planting tender crops. What frost dates really mean

    Why these dates

    Bellingham's typical last spring frost is March 31. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the west-side valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about March 1.

    West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

    Step by step

    1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
    2. Plant seeds Mar 1 – Apr 12, ¼ inch deep, Thin to 4 inches apart.
    3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
    4. Plant again Aug 27 – Sep 17 for a fall harvest.
    5. Harvest: Pick leaves when they're 2 to 3 inches long, about three weeks after sowing. Young leaves are the mildest.

    Varieties that do well here

    • Astro
    • Wild arugula (Sylvetta)
    • Rocket

    Watch out for

    Flea beetles riddle the leaves with tiny holes in spring. Floating row cover laid on at sowing keeps them off.

    Fast and frost-tolerant. It bolts in heat, so sow small batches in spring and again in late summer.

    Common questions

    When is the last frost in Bellingham, WA?

    Typically March 31. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 18. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for Bellingham International Airport.

    Is it too late to plant arugula in Bellingham?

    The spring planting window runs through April 12. If you miss it, plant again for fall from August 27 to September 17.

    How long until I can harvest arugula?

    About 35 days after planting. Pick leaves when they're 2 to 3 inches long, about three weeks after sowing. Young leaves are the mildest.
