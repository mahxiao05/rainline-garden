When to plant vegetables, herbs and berries in the Northwest

Pick a crop for its planting dates in every Oregon and Washington city we cover, plus how to grow it. New to this? Start with the ones marked Easy.

Cool-season crops

Plant in early spring, and many again for fall. They handle light frost.

  • PeasEasy
  • RadishesEasy
  • SpinachModerate
  • LettuceEasy
  • OnionsModerate
  • CarrotsModerate
  • BeetsModerate
  • KaleEasy
  • BroccoliModerate
  • CabbageModerate
  • Swiss chardEasy
  • PotatoesEasy
  • CilantroEasy
  • ParsleyEasy
  • DillEasy

Warm-season crops

Plant after the last frost once the soil warms. Frost kills them.

  • Bush beansEasy
  • Sweet cornModerate
  • CucumbersModerate
  • Zucchini and summer squashEasy
  • Winter squash and pumpkinsModerate
  • TomatoesModerate
  • PeppersTricky here
  • BasilModerate
  • DahliasModerate

Berries

Plant once and pick for years. Hardy through Northwest winters.

  • StrawberriesEasy
  • BlueberriesEasy
  • RaspberriesModerate

Planted in fall

Goes in the ground in autumn and is harvested the next summer.

  • GarlicEasy
