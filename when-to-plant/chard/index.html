When to plant Swiss chard in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant Swiss chard from seed, directly in the garden, Mar 15 – May 10. For a fall harvest, plant again Aug 6–20. In Bend: May 12 – Jul 7. Find your city below.

Difficulty
Easy
Sun
Full sun to part shade
Spacing
10 inches apart
Planting depth
½ inch deep
Frost
Handles light frost
First harvest
About 55 days after planting

Handles both cool spring weather and summer heat; harvest outer leaves for months.

How to grow Swiss chard

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seeds on your city's dates, ½ inch deep, 10 inches apart.
  3. Keep the soil evenly moist until sprouts appear, which can take one to three weeks in cool soil.
  4. Plant again on your city's dates for a fall harvest.
  5. Harvest: Cut outer stalks at the base. One planting keeps producing from early summer into winter.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Bright Lights
  • Fordhook Giant
  • Rhubarb

Watch out for

Leaf miners, the same as beets. Remove spotted leaves early.

What you'll need

  • Bright Lights chard seeds
  • Garden harvest scissors
