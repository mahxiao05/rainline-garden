When to plant potatoes in Oregon and Washington

In Portland: Plant seed potatoes Mar 15 – Apr 26. In Bend: May 12 – Jun 23. Find your city below.

How to grow potatoes

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seed potatoes on your city's dates, 4 inches deep in a trench, 12 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart. Cut large ones into pieces with at least two eyes each.
  3. As plants grow, pile soil or straw around the stems so the potatoes stay covered.
  4. Harvest: Dig new potatoes when the plants flower. For storage potatoes, wait two weeks after the tops die back.

Varieties that do well in the Northwest

  • Yukon Gold
  • German Butterball
  • Red Norland

Watch out for

Wireworms in beds that were recently lawn. Grow potatoes in an established bed if you can.

What you'll need

  • Certified seed potatoes
