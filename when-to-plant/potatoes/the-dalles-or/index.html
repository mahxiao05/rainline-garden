When to plant potatoes in The Dalles, OR

Plant seed potatoes in The Dalles Apr 1 – May 13.

Easy Plant seed potatoes

Why these dates

The Dalles's typical last spring frost is April 11. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about April 1.

Columbia Basin and low eastern valleys. Hot, dry summers and quick spring warm-up. Warm crops go in earlier than west of the Cascades, and irrigation matters more than rain.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seed potatoes Apr 1 – May 13, 4 inches deep in a trench, 12 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart. Cut large ones into pieces with at least two eyes each.
  3. As plants grow, pile soil or straw around the stems so the potatoes stay covered.
  4. Harvest: Dig new potatoes when the plants flower. For storage potatoes, wait two weeks after the tops die back.

Varieties that do well here

  • Yukon Gold
  • German Butterball
  • Red Norland

Watch out for

Wireworms in beds that were recently lawn. Grow potatoes in an established bed if you can.

Use certified seed potatoes. Hill soil around stems as they grow.

What you'll need

  • Certified seed potatoes

Common questions

When is the last frost in The Dalles, OR?

Typically April 11. In 9 years out of 10, frost is over by April 28. These are 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for The Dalles Municipal Airport (Dallesport, WA).

Is it too late to plant potatoes in The Dalles?

The spring planting window runs through May 13. Later plantings tend to struggle in summer heat.

How long until I can harvest potatoes?

About 95 days after planting. Dig new potatoes when the plants flower. For storage potatoes, wait two weeks after the tops die back.
