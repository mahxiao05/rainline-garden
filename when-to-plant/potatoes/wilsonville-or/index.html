When to plant potatoes in Wilsonville and Canby, OR

Plant seed potatoes in Wilsonville and Canby Mar 15 – Apr 26.

Easy Plant seed potatoes

Why these dates

Wilsonville and Canby's typical last spring frost is March 16. As a cool-season crop that handles light frost, this crop doesn't need to wait for that date. In the west-side valleys, though, the soil usually isn't ready to work until about March 15.

West-side valleys. The Willamette Valley, Puget Sound and southern Oregon valleys. Long frost-free seasons, but soil stays cold and wet into May, so warm crops wait for warm soil, not just the last frost.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the bed: loosen the soil and mix in an inch or two of compost.
  2. Plant seed potatoes Mar 15 – Apr 26, 4 inches deep in a trench, 12 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart. Cut large ones into pieces with at least two eyes each.
  3. As plants grow, pile soil or straw around the stems so the potatoes stay covered.
  4. Harvest: Dig new potatoes when the plants flower. For storage potatoes, wait two weeks after the tops die back.

Varieties that do well here

  • Yukon Gold
  • German Butterball
  • Red Norland

Watch out for

Wireworms in beds that were recently lawn. Grow potatoes in an established bed if you can.

Use certified seed potatoes. Hill soil around stems as they grow.
